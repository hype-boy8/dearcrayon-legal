Privacy Policy

Dear Crayon · Effective October 19, 2026

Dear Crayon is an app for parents to record their child’s drawings and voice. It is not designed for children to use on their own; a parent (legal guardian) enters and manages the child’s information.

At a glance

  • All records are stored only on this phone. No account, no developer database, no advertising, analytics or location.
  • The only thing that leaves the phone is voice-to-text, if a parent allows it. The relay server does not store recordings or text.
  • The paid book PDF is bought through Google Play Billing. The developer never receives your card details.

1. What we handle and where it is stored

  • Child’s name, date of birth, artist name and profile photo; photos of artworks; recordings of the child’s voice; what the child said and answered; quotes; parent notes; app settings.
  • All of it is stored only in the app’s storage on this phone. Dear Crayon has no sign-up, no login and no database of its own, and the developer never receives these records.
  • We do not use advertising, usage analytics or location.
  • Android Auto Backup (Google Drive) is turned off. Backups inside the app are files (zip) that a parent creates.

2. When something leaves the phone

  • Voice-to-text (optional): only if a parent allows it at the first recording, the recording to be transcribed and the language setting are sent to the Dear Crayon relay server (Cloudflare Workers). The relay server has the text produced by Cloudflare Workers AI (Whisper speech recognition, Cloudflare, Inc., United States), returns it, and stores neither the recording nor the text. No other records, such as the child’s name, are sent. You can turn this off anytime in Settings › Privacy; when off, nothing is sent.
  • Buying the book PDF: the high-resolution, print-ready book PDF is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment method and billing details; the developer never receives your card details. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase went through and records it on this phone. The developer uses transaction details in the Google Play Console, such as the order number, only to handle refunds and support requests.
  • Files a parent shares: monthly cards, book PDFs, book files and backup files leave the phone only when a parent sends them through an app they choose (messaging, email, etc.). The recipient then has that file.

3. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph artworks.
  • Microphone: to record the child’s voice.
  • Notifications: monthly story and birthday reminders. Everything else still works if you decline.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay on this phone until a parent deletes them.
  • Artworks, answers and quotes can be deleted one by one in the app; deleting a child deletes all of that child’s records and files.
  • Deleting the app deletes every record on this phone. Please delete backup files and shared files yourself wherever you saved them.
  • Because nothing is kept on a server, there is no data to request deletion of separately.

5. Children’s privacy

  • In line with COPPA (US), the GDPR rules for children (EU/UK) and the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, we ask for a parent’s consent before any of the child’s voice is sent to a server.
  • Parents can review, correct or delete the child’s information and withdraw consent at any time, directly in the app.
