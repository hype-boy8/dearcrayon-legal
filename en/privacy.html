Privacy Policy

Dear Crayon · Effective: October 23, 2026

Dear Crayon is an app for parents to record their child’s drawings and voice. It is not designed for children to use on their own; a parent (legal guardian) enters and manages the child’s information.

At a glance

  • Child records stay on the phone; a separate confirmation value and time are stored to confirm teacher-link connections. We do not use advertising, analytics or location.
  • If you turn on Google Drive auto backup (optional), records are also stored in an app-only space in the parent’s own Google Drive. It is off by default.
  • If a school uses the teacher space or a parent uses Connect with teachers (optional), class artwork photos are uploaded locked to the school’s Google Drive and open only in the app of parents who got the link.
  • Parent connection confirmation: When a parent selects a child and completes a teacher-link connection, a separately derived confirmation value and confirmation time are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV for the teacher to check. Names, photos, voice recordings, Drive file IDs and link encryption keys are neither sent nor stored. Historical confirmation may remain after disconnection; this does not track current presence or disconnection in real time. The confirmation record is retained without automatic renewal; contact us below for deletion requests. Cloudflare receives connection information when handling requests.
  • Google Play or Apple App Store handles payments. A separate verification service keeps book and transaction identifiers and purchase times to confirm ownership, without card details or child records.

1. What we handle and where it is stored

  • Child’s name, date of birth, artist name and profile photo; photos of artworks; recordings of the child’s voice; what the child said and answered; quotes; parent notes; app settings.
  • Child records stay in this phone’s app storage, and in the parent’s own Google Drive only if auto backup is enabled. There is no app registration; the developer does not receive or store these child records on a server. Google sign-in is used for Drive backup. Purchase verification data is described separately below.
  • We do not use advertising, usage analytics or location.
  • Android’s system Auto Backup is turned off. The app offers two kinds of backup: files (zip) that a parent creates, and the optional Google Drive auto backup that a parent turns on (off by default).
  • On iPhone, your child’s records (photos, voice and text) are excluded from iCloud and Finder device backups. To move or protect them, use the app’s file backup or Google Drive backup.

2. When something leaves the phone

  • Voice-to-text (optional): only if a parent allows it at the first recording, the recording to be transcribed and the language setting are sent to the Dear Crayon relay server (Cloudflare Workers). The relay server has the text produced by Cloudflare Workers AI (Whisper speech recognition, Cloudflare, Inc., United States), returns it, and stores neither the recording nor the text. No other records, such as the child’s name, are sent. You can turn this off anytime in Settings › Privacy; when off, nothing is sent.
  • Google Drive auto backup (optional): off by default. If a parent signs in with Google and turns it on (“Continue with Google” on the first screen, or My Kids › Backup & export), the same contents as a backup file — child info, artwork photos in original quality, voice recordings, answers and the child’s words, quotes, parent notes and books received from family — are uploaded to a hidden, app-only folder in the parent’s own Google Drive (Google permission drive.appdata). This folder does not appear in the Drive interface; it only uses storage. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive. Data goes directly between the phone and Google Drive (Google LLC); it does not pass through or get stored on any developer server, so the developer cannot see it. By default it uploads only on Wi‑Fi and only while the app is open (a switch allows mobile data). Items deleted in the app are also deleted from the Drive backup at the next upload. The email address of the signed-in account is kept on this phone only, to show which account is used.
  • Teacher space (optional, for preschool and art class teachers): if a teacher connects the school’s Google account, the class name, children’s names and the artwork photos, activity and teacher’s note they take are uploaded to that school account’s Google Drive (Google permissions drive.file — only files this app created, and drive.appdata — class state in a hidden space). Photos and lists are uploaded locked (AES-256) and open only in the app of parents who received the child’s link. Anyone with the link can download the locked files, but they cannot be opened without the key inside the link. Nothing passes through a developer server, so the developer cannot see it. The school is responsible for consent to photograph children and share the photos with their parents. In the school’s Drive, the class and school names appear as folder names; each child’s folder has a number instead of a name. The class state with children’s names and keys is kept in the school account’s hidden app-only space. The school account’s email is stored on this phone only, to show which account is used. If a teacher adds children’s face photos (optional, to recognize them more easily), those photos stay on this phone only and are never uploaded to Drive or sent to parents.
  • Connect with teachers (optional): if a parent connects using the child’s link a teacher sent, the app downloads that link’s list and new artwork photos from Google Drive without signing in, whenever the app opens, and saves them on this phone. Disconnecting stops new downloads; artwork already received can be deleted like any other record. The class and teacher names, activity and teacher’s note are also saved on this phone. When downloading, Google receives the phone’s connection information (IP address), as with any download.
  • Buying the book PDF: Google Play handles payment methods and billing details; the developer does not receive card details. To use a purchase, the app sends its purchase token and an opaque book identifier through the Dear Crayon verification service (Cloudflare Workers) to Google Play. To prevent duplicate use and confirm ownership, the service stores the book identifier, order number, purchase time and verification time. It does not store the raw purchase token or the child’s name, birth date, photos or voice. The purchase journal stays on this phone and is included in backups made by the parent. Order information is used for purchase verification, refunds and support.
  • Book PDF purchases on iOS: Apple App Store handles payment methods; the developer does not receive card details. The app sends an Apple-signed transaction and an anonymous book identifier to a separate Apple verification service on Cloudflare Workers. It verifies Apple’s signature and current transaction/refund state and stores the book identifier, transaction number and purchase time to prevent duplicate use. Raw signed transactions, child names, photos and voice recordings are not stored in this verification ledger. Purchase records are included in parent-created backups.
  • Files a parent shares: monthly cards, book PDFs, book files and backup files leave the phone only when a parent sends them through an app they choose (messaging, email, etc.). The recipient then has that file.
  • Parent connection confirmation: When a parent selects a child and completes a teacher-link connection, a separately derived confirmation value and confirmation time are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV for the teacher to check. Names, photos, voice recordings, Drive file IDs and link encryption keys are neither sent nor stored. Historical confirmation may remain after disconnection; this does not track current presence or disconnection in real time. The confirmation record is retained without automatic renewal; contact us below for deletion requests. Cloudflare receives connection information when handling requests.

3. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph artworks.
  • Microphone: to record the child’s voice.
  • Notifications: monthly story and birthday reminders. Everything else still works if you decline.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay on this phone until a parent deletes them. If Google Drive auto backup is on, the Drive backup also stays in the parent’s Google account until the parent deletes it.
  • Artworks, answers and quotes can be deleted one by one in the app; deleting a child deletes all of that child’s records and files.
  • Deleting the app deletes every record on this phone. The Drive backup remains even if you delete the app, so delete it as described below. Please delete backup files and shared files yourself wherever you saved them.
  • Deleting the Drive backup: “Disconnect account” signs out and stops uploads, but leaves the backup in Drive. Delete backup from Drive in the app deletes the whole Drive backup. Without the app, you can delete it in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Dear Crayon › Delete hidden app data, and revoke access in Google Account › Security › Third-party apps.
  • Child records are not on a developer server, and parents can delete their Drive backup themselves. For questions or deletion requests about separately stored purchase-verification records, contact us below. Deleting those records may limit ownership verification and support.

5. Children’s privacy

  • In line with COPPA (US), the GDPR rules for children (EU/UK) and the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, we ask for a parent’s consent before any of the child’s voice is sent to a server. Google Drive auto backup also works only after a parent signs in and turns it on.
  • Parents can review, correct or delete the child’s information and withdraw consent at any time, directly in the app.
