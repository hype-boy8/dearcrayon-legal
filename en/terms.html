2. The service

The app is a tool for parents to record their child’s drawing photos, voice and words, and to make, view and share monthly cards and books (a year of the child’s records). Records are stored only on the user’s phone.

3. Who can use it

  • The app is for parents and guardians (adults). It is not designed for children to use on their own.
  • Parents record information about the children in their care. Please get permission from anyone else (or their guardian) before adding their photo or voice.

7. Backups are up to you
