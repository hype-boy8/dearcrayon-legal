Got a book from family?

  1. Install the Dear Crayon app with the button above.
  2. Tap the file ending in …dearcrayon.zip you received (in a messaging app or email) and open it with Dear Crayon.
  3. The book opens right away. You can also find it later under View a book from family on the app’s first screen.

About Dear Crayon

Dear Crayon lets parents photograph their child’s drawings and keep, in the child’s own voice, what each drawing is about. Answers to the same monthly questions become a monthly card, and each birthday they are gathered into a book. Everything stays on the phone — no sign-up, no ads.
