Data Deletion

Dear Crayon · Effective October 19, 2026

About this page

This page explains how to delete data in the Android app “Dear Crayon”. Dear Crayon has no Dear Crayon account and no developer server storage. Your child’s name, drawings, voice and words are on your phone and, if you turned on Google Drive auto backup, also in an app-only space in your own Google Drive. You can delete them yourself, immediately, and there is no data the developer could delete on your behalf.

Option 1. Delete individual records

  1. Artwork: open the artwork, tap ⋯ at the top right › Delete artwork › Delete. The photo, voice and story are deleted together.
  2. Answer: on the story screen, open the answer’s menu (⋯) › Delete answer.
  3. Quote: tap the quote › Delete.

Option 2. Delete all of a child’s records

  1. Open the My Kids tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap Edit child info.
  3. Tap Delete child at the bottom and confirm.

All of that child’s artwork photos, voice recordings, answers, quotes and profile photo are deleted and cannot be restored. To keep a copy, first create a file in My Kids › Backup & export.

Option 3. Delete everything in the app

  1. Open your phone’s Settings › Apps › Dear Crayon.
  2. Tap Uninstall, or to keep the app, tap Storage › Clear data.

All records and settings on the phone are deleted immediately. Android’s system Auto Backup is off. However, if you turned on the app’s Google Drive auto backup, the backup in Drive remains — delete it as described in “Delete the Google Drive backup” below.

Delete the Google Drive backup

  1. In the app: My Kids › Backup & export › tap Delete backup from Drive and confirm. The whole Drive backup is deleted.
  2. Without the app: Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Dear Crayon › Delete hidden app data.
  3. Revoke access: Google Account › Security › Third-party apps, then remove Dear Crayon’s access.

Disconnect account signs out and stops uploads, but leaves the backup in Drive. While it is on, records you delete in the app are also deleted from the Drive backup at the next upload.

Turn off voice-to-text

Go to My Kids › ⚙ Settings at the top right › Privacy and turn off Turn voice into text. Recordings will no longer leave the phone. The relay server never stores recordings or text, so there is nothing there to delete.

Files outside the phone

  • Backups and shared files: please delete backup files you created and card or book files you sent to family from wherever you saved them (Files, cloud storage, messaging apps, etc.). Files on a recipient’s phone must be deleted by the recipient.
  • Google Drive backup: if you turned on auto backup, it is in an app-only space in your own Google Drive. Delete it as described in “Delete the Google Drive backup” above.
  • Purchase history: book PDF purchases remain in your Google account’s Google Play purchase history, which you can see under Google Account › Payments & subscriptions.

What is deleted and how long it is kept

Child info, artwork photos, voice, answers, quotes, parent notes, settings
Stored in the app’s storage on the user’s phone. Deleted immediately when you use the options above.
Voice-to-text requests
Not stored on the relay server. Nothing remains once the text is returned.
Google Drive auto backup (if turned on)
Stored in a hidden, app-only space in the user’s own Google Drive (the developer cannot see it). Deleted immediately with “Delete backup from Drive” in the app or “Delete hidden app data” in Google Drive. Items deleted in the app are removed at the next upload.
Records kept on a developer server
None. There is no Dear Crayon account, no developer database, no advertising or analytics tools.
